## Limitations (read first)

1. **Not live execution**: 1h bar OHLC path; same-bar SL+TP uses conservative SL-first.
2. **Per-fold reset capital**: aggregate return ≠ compounded multi-year equity curve.
3. **No funding / no cross-symbol portfolio constraints** (single-symbol level mode).
4. **Label look-ahead truncated** on train end (−84h = look_forward 24 + quantile window 60) so train labels do not peek into the test period.
5. **RF hyperparameters reduced** vs production pickle for walk-forward speed — absolute levels may differ slightly.
6. **Selection bias**: this report is research evidence, not a guarantee of future paper/live PF.
